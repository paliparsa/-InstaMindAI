Privacy

API keys and model lists are stored locally in this browser and are not included in account exports. On review, visible profile text and up to two randomly sampled visible post thumbnails may be sent to Google Gemini only after explicit consent. No gender, identity, age, or other sensitive traits are inferred from photos, names, or faces. No likes, follows, messages, or unfollows are performed.
