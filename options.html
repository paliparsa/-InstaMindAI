AI provider

AI is optional. Your API key is stored in Firefox extension local storage and is not included in exports.

Privacy

No Instagram password, cookies, DMs or private data are collected. Visible profile text and metadata are sent to Gemini only after explicit consent and when you start a review. Saved review consent can be revoked from the review dashboard or by clearing local data. The extension does not infer gender or other sensitive traits and never unfollows automatically.
